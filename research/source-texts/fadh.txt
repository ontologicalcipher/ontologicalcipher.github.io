A proposed hypothesis for frequency-controlled modification of atomic interactions

01 — THE CORE IDEA

FADH proposes a question:

Can a precisely controlled external frequency or stimulus temporarily modify the effective interaction between components of an atomic system?

The original analogy was “anesthesia for atoms” — not literal anesthesia, but a temporary change in the system's behavior under controlled external stimulation.

The proposed sequence is:

External stimulus → altered quantum dynamics → modified effective coupling → possible controlled decoupling

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

02 — THE MATHEMATICAL BASIS

A periodically driven quantum system can be represented as:

H(t) = H₀ + V(t)

For a periodic drive:

V(t) = A cos(ωt)

where:

H₀ = undriven Hamiltonian
A = drive amplitude
ω = angular frequency
V(t) = external time-dependent interaction

In suitable driven two-state/tunnelling models, the effective coupling can take the form:

Jₑff ≈ J × J₀(A/ℏω)

where J₀ is the zeroth-order Bessel function.

At values where:

J₀(A/ℏω) = 0

the model gives:

Jₑff ≈ 0

This demonstrates mathematically that periodic driving can, in specific quantum systems and under the appropriate approximations, strongly suppress an effective coupling.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

03 — THIS PART IS ESTABLISHED PHYSICS

Periodic driving and Floquet engineering are established areas of quantum physics.

Researchers have used periodic drives to engineer effective Hamiltonians and modify tunnelling/interactions in quantum systems, including ultracold atoms.

Therefore, FADH is not claiming to have discovered frequency-controlled quantum coupling modification.

That phenomenon already exists.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

04 — WHERE FADH PROPOSES SOMETHING FURTHER

The hypothesis asks whether the same general principle could be extended toward a different objective:

Could a carefully engineered frequency or stimulus create a temporary state in which a selected atomic interaction becomes sufficiently modified to permit controlled decoupling or manipulation?

That specific extension is not established by the equations above.

It remains the hypothesis.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

05 — THE “ATOMIC ANESTHESIA” ANALOGY

The phrase “atomic anesthesia” describes the intuition behind the idea:

Normal dynamics

↓

Controlled external stimulus

↓

Temporary altered dynamics

↓

Stimulus removed or changed

↓

System returns toward its previous dynamics

This is only an analogy.

It does not mean that atoms can literally be made unconscious or that their fundamental forces can simply be switched off.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

06 — THREE-LEVEL VERIFICATION

LEVEL 1 — MATHEMATICS

SUPPORTED

Periodic Hamiltonians and effective Hamiltonians are mathematically established.

Specific driven quantum models can exhibit strong suppression of effective tunnelling/coupling.

VERDICT: SUPPORTED

LEVEL 2 — EXPERIMENTAL PHYSICS

SUPPORTED IN SPECIFIC SYSTEMS

Floquet engineering and driven quantum systems have been experimentally investigated, including ultracold atomic systems.

VERDICT: ESTABLISHED FOR SPECIFIC QUANTUM SYSTEMS

LEVEL 3 — FADH'S EXTENSION

UNVERIFIED

There is currently no basis to claim that the same mechanism can switch off ordinary atomic binding or separate nuclear constituents.

Periodic driving can also cause heating and eventual thermalization, making control highly dependent on frequency, amplitude, system, and timescale.

VERDICT: OPEN HYPOTHESIS

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

07 — WHAT FADH DOES NOT CLAIM

FADH does not claim:

✗ that frequency alone can turn off nuclear forces

✗ that atoms can simply be separated by applying sound

✗ that nuclear binding can be eliminated without energy input

✗ that unlimited energy can be extracted

✗ that a new physical law has already been discovered

These would require evidence that does not currently exist.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

08 — WHAT WOULD HAVE TO BE SHOWN?

For FADH to progress beyond a conceptual hypothesis, a future model would need to specify:

1. The exact atomic system

2. The interaction being targeted

3. The driving frequency

4. The drive amplitude

5. The predicted effective coupling

6. The timescale over which the modification persists

7. A measurable experimental signature

A successful result would need to be reproducible and distinguishable from ordinary resonance, excitation, heating, ionization, or other known effects.

CONCLUSION

FADH begins with an established physical fact:

External periodic driving can modify quantum dynamics and effective couplings in suitable systems.

The hypothesis then asks whether this principle can be extended into a controllable form of atomic decoupling.

The mathematics supports the possibility of coupling modification in specific models.

It does not yet prove the full FADH mechanism.

That boundary is intentional.

The hypothesis is ours to formulate.
The mathematics tells us what is possible.
Nature decides what is real.
