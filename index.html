04 / Research standard

Separate the claim
from the evidence.

Every investigation should make clear what is known, what follows mathematically, what is proposed and what evidence would change the conclusion.

01

Established knowledge

What accepted theory and reliable evidence currently support.

02

Mathematical results

What follows from definitions, equations and stated assumptions.

03

Proposed hypothesis

The new claim, clearly labelled and precisely defined.

04

Speculation & limits

Unverified interpretations, uncertainties and possible failure points.

05

Experimental tests

What observation or experiment could support or falsify the claim.

05 / About the project

Build ideas.
Test limits.
Keep evidence.

Ontological Cipher is an independent research and systems initiative by Kratik. It brings scientific questions, mathematical modelling, philosophy and technical experimentation into one evolving body of work.

The goal is not to make every idea sound certain. The goal is to make ideas precise enough to inspect, challenge, improve or reject. Research notes should expose their assumptions and distinguish speculation from demonstrated results.

Independent work. Open questions. Transparent reasoning. Evidence before certainty.
