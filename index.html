Hypotheses are not proof
Working principles

Keep the question.
Test the answer.

A good idea should survive contact with evidence. A beautiful explanation is not enough on its own.

01

Separate fact from hypothesis

Distinguish established results, interpretations, and speculation.

02

Make claims testable

State assumptions and look for observations that could prove an idea wrong.

03

Make work reproducible

Preserve methods, calculations, code, and sources so others can inspect the process.

04

Update with new evidence

Confidence should follow evidence—not attachment to an idea.
