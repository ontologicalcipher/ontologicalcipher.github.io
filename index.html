About the journal

Curiosity is a beginning.
Evidence is the standard.

The Ontological Cipher is the independent research identity of Kratik. It brings together scientific questions, mathematical modelling, philosophy and technical experimentation in one public archive.

This is an independent personal project, not a university, laboratory or peer-reviewed journal. Proposed ideas are identified as hypotheses and are not presented as established findings without evidence.
