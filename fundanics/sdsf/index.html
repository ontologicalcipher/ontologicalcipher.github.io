04 / SCIENTIFIC STATUS

What this establishes

Established: the ratio of the two reference distances is approximately 389.17, and multiplying a distance by this dimensionless ratio preserves its unit.

Not established: that the ratio represents a physical law, predicts an independently observable quantity, or provides a new method for measuring star distances.

Further testing would require an independently defined observable and predictions that can be compared against measurements.
